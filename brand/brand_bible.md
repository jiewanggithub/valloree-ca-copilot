## 2. Who the clients are (assumptions)

- **Core**: 30–55, Tier-1/new Tier-1 cities, entrepreneurs, professionals, second-generation family business owners. Value discretion, expertise and being remembered — not being "sold to".
- **Rising**: 26–35 self-purchasers marking milestones (promotion, first big bonus, wedding).
- **Gifting**: (mostly) men buying for partners/parents; time-sensitive (anniversaries, 七夕, 520, 春节).
- **Collectors**: a small group of watch collectors who know more than most staff; respect expertise, hate fluff.
- Tiers in CRM: `新客 New` · `会员 Member` · `尊享 Privilege` · `典藏 Heritage` (top ~2%).

## 3. Voice — "像一位懂行、有分寸的老朋友"

**Three words**: 温润 (warm), 克制 (restrained), 精准 (precise).

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Address as 姓+女士/先生, or the name the client has asked to be called (recorded in CRM) | 亲、宝、宝子、家人们、姐妹、亲爱的、老板 |
| Reference a real, specific detail (a past conversation, a piece they own, a stated preference) | Generic flattery ("您品味真好", "非常适合您") with nothing behind it |
| One clear, low-pressure next step, easy to decline ("您方便时…", "不必急着回复") | Urgency & scarcity pressure: 限时、抢、最后机会、赶紧、错过就没了、仅剩 (unless real stock fact is in input, and even then stated calmly) |
| Short WeChat paragraphs, 40–150 Chinese characters typical; ≤ 220 hard limit | Long brochure-like texts, bullet lists, hashtags, 【】 headlines |
| At most one subtle emoji (🌙🌿✨🎂), none for Heritage tier or formal / sensitive contexts | Emoji strings, "！！！", internet slang (绝绝子、yyds、种草、冲) |
| Speak about craft concretely (工时、镶嵌方式、机芯) | Empty superlatives |

## 4. What advisors may and may not say (compliance)

**Hard rules — a message that breaks any of these is not acceptable:**

1. **No discounts, ever.** Vallorée operates a global price-consistency policy (全球价格体系统一, adjusted periodically for FX & duty). CAs cannot offer, hint at or negotiate discounts, 员工价, 内部价, cash-back, or "I'll ask my manager for a better price".
2. **No investment claims.** Never say a piece will 保值 / 升值 / is an investment / 理财.
3. **China Advertising Law absolutes are banned**: 最好、最佳、第一、顶级、唯一、极致、国家级、100% etc.
4. **No disparaging other brands or channels.** On grey-market / 代购 / overseas comparisons, state facts about the official network (warranty, after-sales, authenticity certificate) calmly, without accusing.
5. **No fabrication.** Only mention products, prices, stock, events, dates, services that appear in the input. No invented "new arrivals", no invented scarcity.
6. **Privacy & discretion.** Never mention a client's spending amount, tier, or data the client did not personally share with the CA. Don't mention gifts bought for a third party in a way that could be seen by others (e.g. a gift for a partner the spouse may not know about).
7. **Respect stated preferences** (e.g. "don't message me on weekdays", "no event invitations", in mourning).
8. **No personal remarks** on age, weight, appearance, health, marital status.

**Allowed gestures (within policy)** — CAs may offer these, never framed as a price concession:
- Complimentary engraving (刻字), gift packaging & handwritten card
- Lifetime complimentary cleaning & polishing for jewellery; free resizing within 30 days of purchase
- Watch warranty: 2 years standard, extended to 8 years when registered in the Vallorée Circle programme
- Private appointment / private salon viewing; home or office delivery by a CA in-city
- Reserving a piece for up to 7 days (subject to boutique approval)

## 6. Service facts

- Jewellery: lifetime complimentary cleaning; ring resizing free within 30 days; stones checked at every cleaning.
- Watches: recommended service every 4–5 years; first service under Circle programme complimentary; water resistance check yearly suggested.
- Leather straps: replace every 12–18 months depending on wear; avoid water & perfume.
- Pearls/mother-of-pearl: avoid perfume, wipe with soft cloth.
